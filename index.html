What is a galaxy?

A galaxy is a huge collection of stars, gas, dust and dark matter bound together by gravity. Small ones hold millions of stars, giants hold trillions, and our Milky Way holds a few hundred billion. The observable universe contains billions of them, and Hubble noticed they come in only a few basic shapes.

The Hubble Sequence

The tuning fork

Ellipticals form the handle, running from round (E0) to stretched (E7). At the fork sit the lenticulars (S0), and from there the spirals split into two prongs: normal spirals above and barred spirals below. Irregulars fit nowhere on it. Hubble drew it only as a sorting scheme and did not claim galaxies evolve along it.
